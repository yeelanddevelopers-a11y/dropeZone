This Privacy Policy explains how DropZone ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. The app is published by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. What DropZone does

DropZone is a guide app for mobile games. It shows game guides, weapons, maps and esports rules, and it lets you adjust a few in-app player settings.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data: technical information such as your device model, operating system version, language, app version, crash and performance information, and your IP address.
  • Advertising identifiers: your device's advertising ID, used by our ad partner to show and measure ads.
  • Settings stored on your device: your in-app preferences are kept on your device and are not sent to us.

3. How we use information

  • To load and display guides, weapons, maps and other content
  • To keep the app working, fix bugs and improve performance
  • To show advertising that helps keep the app free
  • To protect the app against abuse and meet legal obligations

4. Third-party services

The app uses services that may collect information as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising): may use advertising identifiers and device data to show, personalize and measure ads. See policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Firebase (Google): stores the content shown in the app (guides, game data). Firebase may process technical information such as your IP address when the app requests data. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Image and content hosts: images and some content are loaded from the web (for example from dispresso.co), so those servers receive your IP address and request details when images load.

Your ad choices

  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by device).
  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice later.
